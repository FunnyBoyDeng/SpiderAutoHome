# Maintainers

## Primary maintainer

- [@FunnyBoyDeng](https://github.com/FunnyBoyDeng) — project creator, primary maintainer, release and security contact.

## Maintainer responsibilities

The primary maintainer reviews pull requests, triages issues, keeps the supported sample buildable, evaluates dependency and security updates, maintains releases, and records modernization decisions.

Community members who contribute consistently may be invited to become core maintainers. A future core maintainer should demonstrate constructive review, reliable follow-through, and familiarity with the repository's offline-testing and responsible-use requirements.

## Service targets

These are goals rather than guarantees for a volunteer-maintained project:

- acknowledge actionable bug reports within 14 days;
- acknowledge private security reports within 7 days;
- keep CI green on the default branch;
- document breaking changes and release decisions;
- close or re-scope stale roadmap items transparently.

Maintainer automation opportunities and success measures are documented in [docs/MAINTAINER_AUTOMATION.md](docs/MAINTAINER_AUTOMATION.md).
